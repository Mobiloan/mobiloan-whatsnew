# 4.69.13 - Electronic Bank Statements

Mobiloan has introduced an innovative feature that allows for the seamless import of a client's bank statements directly from their bank. This guide outlines the process to utilize this functionality.

#### Integration Partner

* **Gathr Bank Statements** (**Finch Technologies**)- Our third party integration partner enabling this functionality.

#### Step-by-Step Guide

1. **Initiate Import**: During the loan origination process, select the "Import Bank Statements" option when you reach the affordability assessment step.
2. **Authorization**: The client will then be prompted to authorize the import of their bank statement. This can be done through a USSD code or directly via their banking app, ensuring a secure process.
3. **Retrieval**: Once authorized, Mobiloan will automatically retrieve the bank statement from the client's bank. This process reduces the need for manual data entry and minimizes the risk of errors.

#### Benefits

* **Efficient Affordability Assessment**: Accelerate your loan processing by having instant access to the client's bank data.
* **Accuracy in Data**: Avoid manual data entry mistakes and always work with the most current financial information.
* **Enhanced Client Experience**: Make the loan application process smoother and more convenient for your clients through an easy and secure authorization process for sharing their bank statements.
