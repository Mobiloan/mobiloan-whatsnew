# 4.72.05 - Report Synchronization via Google Sheets™️

**Sync Your Mobiloan Reports to Google Sheets:**

Now you can easily connect your Mobiloan reports to Google Sheets. This means you can view, share, and work on your financial data directly in Google Sheets.

**How it works:**

1. **Choose a Report:** Pick the Mobiloan report you want to sync.
2. **Create a Google Sheet:** We'll make a new Google Sheet for you.
3. **Sync Data:** Your Mobiloan data will automatically update in the Google Sheet.
