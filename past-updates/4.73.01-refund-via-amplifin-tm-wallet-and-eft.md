# 4.73.01 - Refund Via Amplifin™️Wallet & EFT

### 🆕 **Introducing Enhanced Amplifin Refund Functionality**

We're excited to announce a new feature that offers a safe and secure way to process EFT and eWallet refunds directly within Mobiloan.

**Key Features:**

* **Role-Based Restrictions:** To ensure security, specific roles will have access to refund functionality.
* **Strict Refund Limits:** Refunds will be limited to the outstanding balance of the loan, preventing overpayments.
* **One Refund per Loan:** Each loan can only have one EFT or eWallet refund processed.
* **Notifications :** You'll receive email notifications for successful and unsuccessful Allps EFT or eWallet refunds.
