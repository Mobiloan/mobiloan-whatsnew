# 4.74.6 - Evaluation Rights for Digital Bank Statements

With the launch of the **Digital Bank Statements** feature, we've introduced a range of new **Evaluation Rights** that correspond to the various **warnings** that may be triggered during a single digital bank statement enquiry.

These rights provide more granular control over how users can interact with, view, or respond to specific warning types generated during evaluations.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (471).png" alt="" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

You can access and configure these rights by navigating to:\
**Setup > Roles > Evaluation Tab**

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (472).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Please review the descriptions of each new evaluation right, and assign them to roles as appropriate, based on your company's compliance requirements.
