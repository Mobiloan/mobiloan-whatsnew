# 4.73.7 -Dynamic Login Audit Log Management

We're excited to introduce the **Login Audit Log Report**, designed to give you insight into user login activity across your business.&#x20;

***

### Outline of the report&#x20;

View the sample data below and note that a chronological sequence of user actions during a session.&#x20;

* **View**: See which screens were accessed, such as "main", "main\_menu", "origination\_status", and more.
* **Duration**: Track how long users spent on each screen.
* **Timestamps**: Pinpoint the exact time of each action during the session.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (23).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

This report now provides a clear, actionable overview of:

* Who logged in (Operator/User).
* Where they logged in from (Branch).
* When they logged in (Login Date and Timestamps).
* What they did during their session (Views and durations).

***

**Example Use Case**

With this feature, you can easily track and analyze login data to enhance operational visibility and ensure accountability.

Imagine wanting to understand how efficiently your team navigates the system. With this report, you can:

* Identify if users spend too much time on certain screens.
* Detect irregular login patterns for security monitoring.
* Ensure compliance with operational protocols.

***

**Accessing the Report:**

Navigate to the **Login Audit Log Report** (Report 120) in Mobiloan's standard reporting suite.
