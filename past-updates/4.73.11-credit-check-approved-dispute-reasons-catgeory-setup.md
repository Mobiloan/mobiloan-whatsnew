# 4.73.11 - Credit Check Approved dispute reasons - Catgeory Setup

We’ve introduced a category configuration that restricts credit check dispute options to a specific set. Please note, if none are selected, credit report disputes will not be allowed. To configure the allowed dispute reasons for this category, go to Setup > Category > Credit Check tab.
