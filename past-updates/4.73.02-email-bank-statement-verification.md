# 4.73.02 - Email Bank Statement Verification

The development team has introduced a new feature that allows clients to conveniently email their bank statements to an **AUTHORIZED shared email address**.&#x20;

The benefits of this feature include :&#x20;

* **No need for physical copies**: Clients no longer need to print and carry a hard copy of their bank statement.
* **Enhanced security**: Enjoy peace of mind knowing that the document has undergone a series of credibility checks.
* **Automatic digitization**: The bank statement is automatically digitized and saved to the client’s profile (referred to as the **Client Menu** in Mobiloan).

***

### What’s required from the client ?

* The client is simply required to **log into** cellphone banking app and generate a bank statement **OR** have a scanned photo / image of the hard copy bank statement ready to send via email.&#x20;
* Once you have a digital copy, email the official bank statement to the shared email address displayed in Mobiloan.

***

### Supported Banks

* ABSA&#x20;
* Capitec&#x20;
* FNB&#x20;
* Standard Bank
* Nedbank&#x20;
* Tyme Bank
* EasyPay

{% hint style="warning" %}
#### Important Notes on EasyPay Banks:

For banks under **EasyPay**, the bank statement may be encrypted with a password, which will be provided to you within your banking app.

To avoid any errors (such as unauthorized access), simply **include the password** in the **subject line** or **email body** when sending the email. This ensures a seamless process.
{% endhint %}

***

### How it works ?

_**See**_ [#requesting-the-emailed-statement](4.73.02-email-bank-statement-verification.md#requesting-the-emailed-statement "mention") _below for screenshots and instructions._

1. During the **Document Capture** step in Mobiloan, select the **“Bank Statement By Email”** option.\
   Alternatively, you can request an emailed statement in the **Client Menu > Documents option.**
2. Request the client to email their bank statement to the **authorized shared email address**. The shared email address will be displayed within Mobiloan and typically ends with **@getmydoc**.
3. In Mobiloan, select  the **“Bank Statement has been emailed”** toggle switch, and thereafter select the "**Check Inbox**" button, which will listen for the incoming email.&#x20;
4. Once received, the bank statement will be transformed into a digitalized version and stored on the client profile.
5. Proceed with the loan application process.&#x20;
6. Later, the bank statement will be available in the **Client Menu** for future reference.\
