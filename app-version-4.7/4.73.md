## 4.73.61 – Automatic Inclusion of Active Installments in Bureau Accounts Expense

_Released: TBD_

### 🚀 New Features

We’ve introduced a new **Category setting** that automatically includes any active installments in the **Bureau Accounts Expense** calculation.

**Why This Matters?**

Previously, some loans failed to reflect up-to-date installments, leading to disputes and inaccurate affordability assessments. With this new setting, **Mobiloan** ensures that all **active installments** within the system are considered—eliminating errors and reducing the need for manual adjustments.

**How It Works**

* If a client has an **active previous installment** and takes a **top-up loan**, Mobiloan will automatically factor in the existing installment when assessing affordability.
* This is particularly useful if a **recent credit check** has already been performed, and you don’t want to create a **new bureau inquiry**.
* The system pulls the **most recent installment data** directly from the **Client Ledger** and integrates it into the **Bureau Accounts** field.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (454).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**How to Enable This Setting**

1. Navigate to **Setup Menu > Category > Affordability Tab**.
2. Enable the checkbox for **"Auto-Include Active Installments"**.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (453).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

This enhancement helps maintain accurate affordability assessments while reducing manual intervention.

## 4.73.14 – Section 129 Notice Improvements

_Released: TBD_

### 🔧 Improvements

We’ve updated our contract generation process to include **improved and compliance-aligned wording** that meets recent regulatory requirements. Here’s what’s changed:

**Updated Default Notice Wording**

Contracts now include a **clear and compliant default notice** in line with Section 129(1)(a) of the National Credit Act (NCA). The notice provides clients with detailed information about their default status and outlines the steps they can take to resolve the issue.

The updated wording includes:

* A clear statement of the default and the outstanding balance.
* A proposal for resolving the default within **10 business days**.
* Options for the client to contact the business, seek assistance, or refer the matter to a debt counsellor or dispute resolution agent.
* A reminder of the potential consequences if the default is not resolved within **20 business days**, including legal enforcement.

> In terms of section 129(1)(a) of the NCA, your attention is hereby drawn to the fact that you are in default with your obligations under the credit agreement with account number **loan\_number** that you entered into with the business.\
> The arrear amount is **balance\_due**\
> In terms of section 129, read together with section 130 of the NCA, the business proposes that you:
>
> 1. Pay the default amount into your account within 10 (ten) business days of receipt of this notice.
> 2. Contact the business, either in person, on telephone number , or via email at **branch\_address** to assist you in developing a mutually agreeable plan to bring the arrear payment(s) under the loan agreement up to date.
> 3. Refer the above credit agreement to a debt counsellor, alternative dispute resolution agent, consumer court, or ombud with jurisdiction with the intent to resolve any dispute under the agreement.\
>    Should you fail to respond to this notice within 10 (ten) business days from delivery hereof by either rejecting the aforesaid proposal or by failing to respond to this notice at all and should you remain in default with your obligations as aforesaid for a period of 20 (twenty) business days since your default commenced, the credit agreement may be cancelled and we will be entitled to proceed with legal steps to enforce the agreement, which includes issuing a summons against you in a court with the necessary jurisdiction.

This update ensures that your contracts are **fully compliant** with the NCA and provide clients with clear, actionable information to resolve defaults.

## 4.73.9 – Late Settlement Fees

_Released: TBD_

### 🚀 New Features

We’re excited to introduce the **Late Costs Settlement Feature**—a powerful new feature designed to help automate and streamline the tracking and application of late fees within Mobiloan.

This monitor can be fully **configured and tailored to individual loan products**, allowing lenders to define how and when late settlement costs should be applied.

Once configured, **Mobiloan will automatically accrue a Late Settlement amount** whenever a loan becomes overdue, clearly reflecting the amount owed by the client.

#### **How It Works**

* A **cloud-based task runs daily at 12:00 AM**, scanning all loans for overdue installments.
* When overdue loans are identified, **late fees are automatically generated** based on the configuration applied to the loan product - as **pending fees.**
* If the user chooses to **initiate a Late Settlement**, Mobiloan will automatically **generate a new scheduled installment** that reflects the applicable **late fees**. This installment will be added to the client’s **overall loan balance**, ensuring the outstanding amount is accurately updated to include the accrued charges.
* Even if a client has paid off their final scheduled installment via debit order, **late interest and service fees may still be outstanding** if any payments were made after their due dates.
* These unpaid fees remain on the **loan ledger**, and the client’s status will continue to show as **Overdue** or under **Management Hold** until a Late Settlement is completed.
* These additional **late settlement fees** can be either **Captured manually** through a direct payment method (e.g. cash or EFT) **or,** If the fees are **substantial** and the original **mandate has expired**, they can be **included in the next debit order mandate** created for the client.

This ensures that outstanding amounts are recovered in a manner that is both accurate and aligned with the client’s current repayment setup.

#### **Important Behavior Notes**

* If this feature is enabled **starting today**, it will only begin to calculate late fees based on **current month’s installments** or those that are **scheduled within the current month**.
* At **month-end**, Mobiloan will begin to **accrue late fees** for any installments that have passed their scheduled date.
* **Previously late installments that have already been paid** will not be retroactively included in the calculation of new late fees.

For more information on how to configure Late Costs in your loan products, please select the links below.

## 4.73.02 – Email Bank Statement Verification

_Released: TBD_\
&#xNAN;_&#x50;owered by Finch Technologies_

### 🚀 New Features

The development team has introduced a new feature that allows clients to conveniently email their bank statements to an **AUTHORIZED shared email address**.

The benefits of this feature include :

* **No need for physical copies**: Clients no longer need to print and carry a hard copy of their bank statement.
* **Enhanced security**: Enjoy peace of mind knowing that the document has undergone a series of credibility checks.
* **Automatic digitization**: The bank statement is automatically digitized and saved to the client’s profile (referred to as the **Client Menu** in Mobiloan).

#### What’s required from the client ?

* The client is simply required to **log into** cellphone banking app and generate a bank statement **OR** have a scanned photo / image of the hard copy bank statement ready to send via email.
* Once you have a digital copy, email the official bank statement to the shared email address displayed in Mobiloan.

#### Supported Banks

* ABSA
* Capitec
* FNB
* Standard Bank
* Nedbank
* Tyme Bank
* EasyPay

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Important Notes on EasyPay Banks:**

For banks under **EasyPay**, the bank statement may be encrypted with a password, which will be provided to you within your banking app.

To avoid any errors (such as unauthorized access), simply **include the password** in the **subject line** or **email body** when sending the email. This ensures a seamless process.
{% endhint %}

#### How it works ?

1. During the **Document Capture** step in Mobiloan, select the **“Bank Statement By Email”** option.\
   Alternatively, you can request an emailed statement in the **Client Menu > Documents option.**
2. Request the client to email their bank statement to the **authorized shared email address**. The shared email address will be displayed within Mobiloan and typically ends with **@getmydoc**.
3. In Mobiloan, select the **“Bank Statement has been emailed”** toggle switch, and thereafter select the "**Check Inbox**" button, which will listen for the incoming email.
4. Once received, the bank statement will be transformed into a digitalized version and stored on the client profile.
5. Proceed with the loan application process.
6. Later, the bank statement will be available in the **Client Menu** for future reference.
