## 4.74.5 – Allps Registered Mandate Payment Stream

_Released: TBD_

### 🚀 New Features

Amplifin’s latest release, **the RMAN (Registered Mandate) payment stream** , has introduced major structural improvements — and as a result, we've adapted our Mobiloan platform to support this more robust and standardized payment flow.

Please take the time to read the articles in Amplifin's RMS to RM campaign for more information:

{% embed url="https://amplifin.co.za/navigating-the-shift-how-south-africas-debit-order-landscape-is-transforming/" %}

{% embed url="https://amplifin.co.za/the-advantages-of-registered-mandate-rm-in-modernising-south-africas-debit-order-system/" %}

#### Registered Mandate as a Repayment Method in Mobiloan

You can now configure **Registered Mandate (RMAN)** as a repayment method within Mobiloan.\
This opens up the ability for users to create **Promissory Notes** specifically tied to new RMAN mandate creations — providing a more seamless, reliable, and compliant debit order process.

**When setting up a new repayment method**, simply select **“Registered Mandate”** from the **Payment Stream** dropdown.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (468).png" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Once selected, an additional dropdown will appear, allowing you to configure the default debit order settings — including the **RMAN Mode** to be used when generating RMS Promissory Mandates.

The RMAN Mode options are:

* **RMAN Online** – Initiates the mandate creation process online, requiring an active internet connection.
* **RMAN Offline** – Triggers mandate creation via a bank-issued instruction, processed through the Allps system without the need for an internet connection.

**For more information on setting up a Repayment Method, please click the link below:**

{% embed url="https://docs.mobiloan.io/setup-and-config/setup-menu/repayment-method-setup#configurable-fields-for-debit-order-payment-streams" %}

#### **Integration Enhancements**

To support the updated RMAN payment structure, key enhancements have been made within the **internal Allps integration framework**.&#x20;

These updates ensure that the system can now seamlessly detect, create, and manage **RMAN-specific mandate creation flows**.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (469).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### **Deprecated Role Updates**

The **“Create RMS Transaction”** role item has been **removed** from the **Debit Order Rights** section within the **Role Setup**.

This change reflects that **RMAN is now the primary mechanism** for mandate creation within the system.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (470).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### Wrap Up&#x20;

* **RMS Discontinuation**: The **Register Mandate Service (RMS)** will be discontinued from **10 March 2025**.
* **New Payment Stream**: A new stream, **Registered Mandate (RM)**, will replace RMS in the **National Payment System**.
* **Mandate Timing**: RM mandates can only be created **from 10 March 2025** onwards.
* **Migration**: All existing **RMS mandates** will be **migrated to RM** and processed under RM rules.
* **Simplified Process for Amplifin Clients**: Clients can create RM mandates/collections **without needing a failed DebiCheck attempt**.
* **Bank Compatibility**: RM works with **all EFT Debit-compatible accounts**.
* **Priority Presentation**: RM collections will be **presented before EFT Debits**.
* **Tracking Support**: RM includes **tracking**, starting from **12:00 each day**.
* **Real-Time Responses**: Settlements occur on the **same day**, with **near real-time responses**, similar to DebiCheck.
* **Account Types**: RM supports **juristic accounts** and those requiring **multiple signatories**.
* **Infrastructure**: RM uses the **same mandate infrastructure as DebiCheck**, though **collections are handled separately**.
* **Mandate Flexibility**: RM mandates can be **upgraded to DebiCheck**, and vice versa.

For more details, read up below or feel free to reach out to the support tech team at Mobiloan Support

## 4.74.4 – Email Digital Bank Statements

_Released: TBD_

### 🚀 New Features

We’re excited to introduce a new feature that makes the retrieval of electronic bank statements possible via email.&#x20;

Thanks to our third-party integrator, **Gathr Bank Statements** (powered by Finch Technologies), Clients can now email their bank statement to a specific shared email address provided by the Mobiloan support team (e.g., abcfinance@getmydoc.co.za).&#x20;

#### **Email Bank Statements To:**

The email address will be displayed within Mobiloan and will typically include your **Trade Name** followed by **@getmydoc**.

#### How to share a statement via email

Request the client to send their digital PDF bank statement to the provided email address. Ensure that their ID number is included as the **subject** of the email for easy identification.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (461).png" alt="" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Once the email is received, Gathr will perform a fraud check on the bank statement and then import the bank transactions onto the client’s profile.

Also - if the pdf contains a scanned image - and the image quality is too poor to determine the validity of the document- Gathr will not accept the bank statement.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (462).png" alt="" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Once the fraud test is completed, and the client's bank account is already set up within the system, the statement and bank transactions will be automatically linked to their profile.

You can verify this by navigating to the **Client Step** of the Origination Workflow. If you’ve correctly added the client’s ID number as the subject of the email, Mobiloan will automatically populate the bank account information in the client’s profile.

#### User Guide Documentation

To find out more about how to obtain digital bank statements and manage them within Mobiloan, please refer to the User Guide.

## 4.74.3 – Asset Register

_Released: TBD_

### 🚀 New Features

We’re excited to introduce the new **Asset Register** feature, now available in the Tools Menu of Mobiloan. This feature provides a comprehensive inventory that helps you track and manage all the assets owned by your business, whether they are physical or intangible.

The **Asset Register** is designed to help you manage a wide range of assets, from equipment and vehicles to property, intellectual property, and financial investments. It ensures that you can keep an organized record of everything your business owns, and streamline your asset management process.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (466).png" alt="" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### User Guide Documentation&#x20;

To learn how to create an asset and explore other features of the **Asset Register**, please refer to the **User Guide** documentation available on our website .

{% embed url="https://docs.mobiloan.io/main-menu/tools-menu/asset-register" %}

The guide will walk you through the process of adding assets to your register (shown below), managing existing assets, and utilizing advanced options for efficient asset management.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (467).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### **Key Functions of the Asset Register**&#x20;

1. **Asset Tracking**:\
   Maintain detailed records for each asset, including essential details like purchase date, cost, location, and condition. This allows for easier management and real-time updates of your asset portfolio.
2. **Depreciation Management**:\
   Track the depreciation of assets over time for accurate financial reporting and tax purposes. This helps ensure compliance with accounting standards and provides accurate data for your business’s financial records.
3. **Compliance and Audits**:\
   The Asset Register ensures that your business adheres to accounting standards and regulations. It also provides the necessary documentation for audits, making the process seamless and straightforward.
4. **Attachment Uploads**:\
   Upload photos or documents related to each asset, ensuring you have comprehensive documentation. This makes it easier to access important files and maintain thorough records for every asset listed.
