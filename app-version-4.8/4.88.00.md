# 4.88 Release notes

## 4.88.00 - Staff Screening, FIC Reporting & Collection Controls

_Released: 2026-09-09_

Mobiloan **4.88.00** builds on **4.87.00** (5 August 2026). Below is what your branches, agents and administrators will notice — summarised for day-to-day use.

**Before rollout:** review **Setup → Role** and **Setup → Configuration** for the items in the table at the end. After role or configuration changes, staff should fully close and reopen the app. Devices still running an older version will be asked to update before a loan can be signed.

### 🚀 New

**Choose how wallet and EFT commission gets paid out** — If a role has **Payout agent commission** and/or **Payout broker commission**, you can now also give it **Pay wallet/EFT commission immediately** on **Setup → Role → Tools**. With that switched on, paying an agent's wallet or EFT commission offers **Pay now** or **Record for reconciliation**; without it, the payout stays **Record Commission**, exactly as before. An immediate payment still shows up on the next cashbox reconciliation for balancing — it just isn't paid through Allps a second time. Cash and payroll commission are unaffected; they were already paid immediately.

**Report a loan product as a SACRRA Student Loan** — A loan product's **Product** tab now has a **Report as SACRRA student loan** toggle, visible to support users. Switch it on for a product whose loans should report to SACRRA as a Student Loan account instead of a Personal Loan account; every other product keeps reporting exactly as it does today.

**Staff sanctions screening** — Operators and agents can now be screened against the sanctions list on the same rules already used for clients. A clear screen saves the person and records the check; a match blocks a new operator or agent from being created, and on someone already on the system deactivates or archives them and emails compliance. Operator and agent detail gain a **TFS Screening History** table. Staff are also covered by the bulk rescreen run. **You must switch this on:** enable FIC, then **Operator TFS Screening** and/or **Agent TFS Screening** under **Setup → Configuration → FIC KYC → Staff TFS Screening**. Both are off until you turn them on, and turning FIC off clears them.

**Due Diligence Level on risk relevance rules** — Risk Relevance Rules gain an optional **Due Diligence Level** column — Simplified, Standard or Enhanced — for the FICA classification your RMCP Annexure B4 needs. It sits after FIC Enquiry and is separate from it: changing one does not change the other, it is never required, and existing rules stay blank until someone chooses a value. Where a matching rule has a level, it is stored against the completed Client Due Diligence and shown on the KYC PDF.

**Export your FIC settings to Excel** — **Export FIC settings** now sits beside Overall Maximum Score Estimates on **Setup → Configuration → FIC KYC**, while FIC Required is on. It downloads a workbook with two sheets — your Risk Relevance Rules and your Assessment Groups — for review or for an RMCP annexure. It only reads; nothing in your FIC setup is changed.

**Require an Allps terminal for POS payments** — A new role option, **Require Allps terminal for POS (USB or remote)**, blocks manual POS capture for that role. With it on, the manual **POS Reference** field is hidden and the payment cannot be saved until the operator picks the USB or the remote terminal. Editing a POS payment already recorded is unaffected, and taking a second payment in the same sale needs its own terminal selection. **You must grant it deliberately:** it is left out of every "select all" on the role's rights menu, and Super User and Master User always keep every POS option.

**Pause automatic collection ticket updates** — When a client agrees to pay on a date, hourly processing of failed debit orders used to keep re-opening and re-dating the collection ticket underneath the operator. Operators with the new **Pause automatic collection ticket updates** right can set a **Pause collection tickets until** date on the collection screen; until that date, failed debit orders leave the ticket's status, amounts, dates and action history alone, and **the day after the date normal behaviour resumes on its own**. A **Clear** action ends a pause early and records why on the ticket. A record of each debit order attempt is still saved and clients are still placed on hold for insufficient funds or a dispute — a pause suspends the ticket chasing, not the hold. **You must enable the right on the intended roles and have those operators log out and back in**, otherwise the button does not appear.

**Colleague on the next-of-kin Relation list** — The Relation dropdown on a client reference now offers **Colleague**. Existing next-of-kin records keep the relation already stored. A Colleague is not treated as a spouse or a dependant, and ID and date of birth stay category-driven, exactly as they are for Friend.

**F5 reloads the screen on desktop** — Pressing **F5** on a desktop now reloads the screen you are on, doing exactly what **Reload page** on the hamburger menu does — useful while waiting for documents to upload. Phones are unchanged.

### ✨ Improved

**A warning when the instalment frequency does not match the client** — On **Origination → Product**, choosing an instalment frequency that differs from the client's occupation payday or from their recent loan history now asks to confirm, for example: "Client occupation is weekly and previous loans were weekly. You selected monthly. Continue?" **Continue** carries on; **Cancel** puts the frequency back, or stops the calculation. The answer is remembered only for that exact combination, so a changed employer or newly synced loan history asks again. Two related corrections travel with it: switching to a product that does not allow the frequency you had selected now resets it to one the product does allow — which also restores the maximum number of instalments the product enforces — and employers paid "Monthly by rule" now select a monthly frequency instead of leaving the dropdown blank. SASSA and government-pension originations keep their forced frequency with no new question.

**Fetch missing payments is offered as soon as ALLPS shows the collection completed** — On **Promissory → Instalment → Bank Responses**, the fetch button used to stay hidden until the tracking grace period elapsed, even when ALLPS already showed the instalment as collected. Operators in that position now see the fetch prompt straight away instead of the grace message. Instalments that ALLPS still shows as tracking or incomplete wait out the grace period exactly as before.

**Branches that never finished setup no longer clutter branch lists** — A branch someone started and walked away from is no longer offered when choosing a branch on an application, a collection, a voice call, a repayment method, an Allps terminal, an insurance branch, a Sudonum number, a remote terminal profile or the NCR certificate viewer. Branches that completed setup are unaffected, and an abandoned setup still holds its branch reference so a new branch cannot take it.

**Branches can share an insurance branch code** — Branch Setup now lets more than one branch use the same insurance branch code, where your insurer has set your branches up that way. Before, a second branch with a code already in use could not be saved. Each branch's own branch reference must still be unique, and a blank insurance branch code is still filled in for you from the branch reference. **Check** that each branch's insurance branch code matches how your insurer has set up your branches.

**Account verification works where it previously refused to run** — The AVS button no longer requires a provider token that some instances never had, so account verification is available on those instances.

**A first-time operator login is guided instead of refused** — An operator logging in for the first time now gets a welcome flow and a cellphone OTP rather than a "password invalid" dead end.

**Smaller things you will notice** — Address fields accept commas and line breaks. A phone number returns to its usual `0xx…` display when a save is abandoned. Card numbers are capped at 16 digits and paydate tracking dates at ten days from the first debit date. Where a branch has no Allps terminal, the bank account number check (check-digit validation) is skipped with a notice instead of failing quietly, and an account that needs reverse account verification (bank-initiated check) no longer detours through the bank account screen.

**Emailed remittances carry the commission total, and keep their receipt date** — Emailing a payroll remittance from the history list now includes **Commission Total**, matching the PDF and the in-app header. Republishing a remittance that had already been receipted no longer blanks its **Date Receipted**. Remittances published before this release simply omit the commission row rather than showing a misleading zero.

### 🛠️ Fixed

- **Fetch Missing Payments** keeps every bank response it has already retrieved when a later page fails or the fetch is interrupted, instead of discarding them — those responses could not be requested again, so the payments they carried left loans looking unpaid. An interrupted fetch also no longer resets the search date and skips everything earlier.
- **Two clients can no longer be issued the same client number** when the number counter fails to save. If a signature is interrupted, the operator now gets a clear "action required" message and can continue, instead of being blocked by an "active loan in progress" message on the next attempt.
- A **credit enquiry no longer submits a previous client's passport**, which put the wrong bureau file against a client. Alongside it, the country codes for **Madagascar, Mauritius and Seychelles** are corrected: Mauritian clients stop being asked to update all passport details on every save, their passport reaches the credit enquiry and the Allps mandate, and the foreign-national payout alert fires for them.
- **When the device is offline, the app no longer answers with the wrong result.** A loan that exists is no longer reported as **No Match**, a client with an address is no longer told **Address Required**, a credit enquiry already on file is no longer reported as missing (which invited a second billable bureau request), and a Collection tile no longer falls back to listing every collection in the company. The app now says the connection failed.
- **A call that never reached the server no longer reports a transaction outcome.** Most importantly, an interrupted electronic payout no longer shows **Payout Failed** as a statement of fact when the payout may have gone through — it now warns plainly that the outcome is unknown. A marketing SMS campaign whose opt-out list cannot be checked now stops and says so, instead of sending to the full list.
- A **signature or portrait still uploading** is no longer cleared during login remediation, and a document replacement can no longer be dropped from a save.
- A **client with no bank statement is no longer shown the previous client's transactions** and fraud flags. First-time login can no longer send the OTP to a different operator who shares a birthdate, and a remote terminal can no longer be assigned to a branch that has no terminal.
- An **agent's commission is no longer armed for payout before its transactions exist** — the payout could fire with nothing to pay and say nothing. Undoing a reconciliation no longer reports success after a failure, and a protected record whose linked records fail to delete is no longer half-deleted.
- **Account verification no longer blocks payout because a client's first name has more than one word.** Those clients also stop picking up an "initials do not match" risk reason on the FIC report, so Branch Managers no longer have to escalate them.
- **Allps client sync** no longer creates a duplicate enrolled client for someone whose ID or passport appears on more than one record. Where it cannot tell the records apart safely, it now skips and reports instead of guessing.
- **Reversing a paid-out loan** (Reversed / Cooling Off Settlement) no longer leaves the instalment schedule behind on the loan.
- **Close Loan** can no longer mark a loan **Early Settlement** while a balance is still outstanding.
- **SASSA DebiCheck alignment** no longer sends a tracking period the bank rejects, which had left later months collecting on the wrong day. Alignment now reports failure when any instalment did not succeed, and retries rather than silently settling for a shorter tracking window.
- A **digital bank statement arriving by email** no longer creates a bank account with an unrecognised bank, which showed as an invalid value in the Bank Accounts list and made opening or adding an account fail. Where a bank cannot be identified, no account is created and the problem is reported. Accounts already affected are repaired automatically when a later statement arrives for a recognised bank; a small number of older records need support to correct.
- **Allps accounts** opens fully again — profile, payout accounts and payout history — instead of failing part-way through loading the history.
- **Client Ledger** no longer hangs when opening certain overpaid loans.
- **Republishing a payroll remittance** no longer serves the previous file when viewing, downloading or emailing it in the minutes afterwards, and desktop **Print** and **View PDF** work correctly with the new links.
- The nightly **Payout Deduction Removal** email no longer repeats the same rows every night. Rows already reported to the branch are cleaned up without being listed again.
- The **daily missing-documents sweep** now runs at 04:00 and covers the whole previous day, so payouts made late in the evening and on Sundays are picked up, including loans whose contract had not yet been produced.
- The **nightly Allps client sync** no longer fails on branches whose details were copied from another record, and a failure on one branch no longer stops every branch after it in the same run.
- Saving **Google Sheets sync settings** on a custom report no longer creates a new spreadsheet — the original shared workbook keeps updating.
- **Changing a category's Client Type** away from SASSA or Government Pensioner now clears the leftover Fixed Employer, so Client Occupations stop auto-assigning it and a real employer can be set as Default. Audit records for these saves now list only the field that actually changed, record a change once rather than twice, and show the related record's name instead of blank text. Three Affordability fields that Save already treated as compulsory now show the asterisk that marks them required.
- An **in-app SQL report that returns no rows** now shows **No matching results found** instead of closing with nothing, and a queued report that finds nothing still notifies.
- **Reconcile → Data Summary** shows **Sum** and **Average** again, including for Amount. Columns that net to zero show `0` rather than a dash.
- **Branch Setup → Amplifin** renders its organisation, branch, inscription, workstation and profile fields again instead of showing a blank tab below the toggles.
- **Authorized Branches** shows again on **User → Access** when editing an existing operator.
- **Help desk tickets raised by WhatsApp, email or the website** open without error. Actions that cannot yet be performed on those tickets are explained rather than failing with an unclear message.
- **Pressing back on an archived record** no longer asks whether to save; restoring stays the separate **Save and Restore** action. Active records still prompt as before.
- **Logging out** no longer raises an internal error alert on a logout that in fact succeeded.
- **Son-in-law** and **Daughter-in-law** display correctly on next-of-kin records, and an affordability PDF no longer fails to produce when a reference has no relation recorded — which previously took the whole payout document run down with it.

### 🔒 Reliability and security

**Fetch Missing Payments now checks branch access on the server.** An operator can only fetch for a branch they are authorised for; a device that is not sees **Branch Not Authorised** and nothing is fetched or saved. Operators who are exempt from branch restriction today — support devices, the master operator, Super User — and operators with no restriction configured are unaffected, and a normal authorised fetch is unchanged.

**Stored credentials are protected by a stronger key.** Operator surephrases and stored third-party credentials now use a strengthened encryption key. Nothing already saved stops working and there is no migration window — records move across as they are next saved.

This release also carries build, testing and internal infrastructure work that has no effect on day-to-day use.

### Before you go live

| What to check | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Turn on staff screening for operators and agents | **Setup → Configuration → FIC KYC → Staff TFS Screening** |
| Notification email for the bulk screening run (optional) | **Setup → Configuration → FIC KYC** |
| Billing rate for staff screening, if you bill for it | **Setup → Billing** |
| Due Diligence Level on each risk relevance rule (optional) | **Setup → Configuration → FIC KYC → Risk Relevance Rules** |
| Export FIC settings — anyone who can open Configuration can export; there is no separate permission | **Setup → Configuration → FIC KYC** |
| Who must use an Allps terminal for POS | **Setup → Role → Transaction → Allps Point-of-sale → Require Allps terminal for POS (USB or remote)** |
| Who may pause automatic collection ticket updates | **Setup → Role → Collection Rights → Pause automatic collection ticket updates** |

Branches need no install — the release is applied centrally. After deploy, force-close and reopen the app (or log out and back in) so new settings and permissions sync cleanly. The app updates itself over the air, so staff should expect an update prompt on their device the next time they open Mobiloan — that prompt is expected and should be accepted. Operators granted the new collection or POS rights must log out and back in before the new controls appear.
