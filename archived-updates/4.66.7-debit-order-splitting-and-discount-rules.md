# 4.66.7 Debit Order Splitting and Discount Rules

We have included a new feature in Loan Origination called the [**Debit Order Splitting**](https://docs.modalityapps.com/modules/origination/payout/3.-contract#debit-order-contract-splitting) feature which allows clients that meet the given criteria the option to split their contracts.

There has been a modification to the data model to facilitate the addition of the **Discount Rule Feature in Mobiloan.**

A discount rule belongs to a single **employer**, and has many loan products. In the configuration of a Loan Product, one may include several discount rules, that may be applicable.
