# 4.69.11 - Multiple Insurance Policies, Compulsory credit life

We have made changes to the codebase so that you can **add more insurance products to loan products**, credit life insurance can also be compulsory.

End users with Insurance policy's integrated can now easily download an **Insurance Report** from the **Advanced Reporting** sub menu in the Reporting Module.&#x20;

This report is based on the following policies:&#x20;

1. Aspis (_policies issued **or** premiums receipted_).
2. Groups R Us.
3. Loanfin (_Credit Life **or** Voluntary Life)._&#x20;
