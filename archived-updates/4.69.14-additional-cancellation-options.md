# 4.69.14 - Additional Cancellation Options

## How to use this new feature

After recognizing the need for reversing loans without cancelling the promissory, we've completed the development and made improvements to provide more options to the user when needing to reverse and  cancel a loan.

&#x20;These include select a reversal actions such as&#x20;

* reverse payout only or
* &#x20;reverse payout and cancel the loan and promissory.&#x20;

Also, Mobiloan will detect that a receipt has been posted on the loan you are trying to cancel and will present the appropriate warning message .
