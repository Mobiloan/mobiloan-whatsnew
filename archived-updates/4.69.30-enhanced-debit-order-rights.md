# 4.69.30 - Enhanced Debit Order Rights

We've enhanced the access control over which actions may be performed when creating / editing an active promissory.&#x20;

The table below outlines the rights that may be find in `Setup >> Role >> Transaction Rights`

<table><thead><tr><th width="255">Right</th><th>Description</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Suspend or activate promissory</td><td>The ability to temporarily pause or restart a series of scheduled debit orders.</td></tr><tr><td>Cancel any promissory</td><td>The ability to permanently stop a series of scheduled debit orders.</td></tr><tr><td>Split initial promissory</td><td>The ability to divide an initial debit order amount into multiple installments.</td></tr><tr><td>Edit promissory repayment type</td><td>The ability to change the way a series of debit orders are repaid.</td></tr><tr><td>Edit promissory bank account</td><td>The ability to change the bank account from which a series of debit orders are drawn.</td></tr><tr><td>Edit promissory first debit date</td><td>The ability to change the start date for a series of debit orders.</td></tr><tr><td>Edit promissory periods</td><td>The ability to change the frequency of debit orders in a series (e.g., monthly to weekly).</td></tr><tr><td>Edit promissory installment frequency</td><td>The ability to change how often installments are paid within a series of debit orders.</td></tr><tr><td>Edit promissory collection day rule</td><td>The ability to define the specific day of the month when debit orders in a series are collected.</td></tr><tr><td>Edit promissory date adjustment rule</td><td>Potentially the ability to define how the due date is adjusted for weekends or holidays (specific functionality may vary).</td></tr><tr><td>Edit installment date</td><td>The ability to change the due date of a specific debit order within a series.</td></tr><tr><td>Edit installment amount</td><td>The ability to change the amount of a specific debit order within a series.</td></tr><tr><td>Edit tracking days</td><td>Potentially the ability to define a buffer period between the notification of a debit order and its collection (specific functionality may vary).</td></tr><tr><td>Suspend installment</td><td>The ability to temporarily pause a specific debit order within a series.</td></tr><tr><td>Add new installment</td><td>The ability to add an additional instalment</td></tr><tr><td>Create an RMS transaction</td><td>Register Mandate for failed authentications.</td></tr></tbody></table>
