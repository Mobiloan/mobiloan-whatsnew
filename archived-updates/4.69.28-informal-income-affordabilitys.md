# 4.69.28 - Informal Income Affordability's

Informal income earners are REQUIRED to select a predefined INFORMAL INCOME CATEGORY from an NCR Approved Standard Categories regarded as INFORMAL INCOME .&#x20;

That's why we've expanded our system to include over 1**00 informal income categories** that you can choose from during the affordability assessment process (when declaring the informal income amount) .

&#x20;Additionally, you can now configure the minimum, maximum, and rounding settings for each category for even greater flexibility.
