# 4.71.00 - Automatic handovers and write-offs

Mobiloan now offers automated handover and write-off features for loans qualifying under Section 129(a) of the National Credit Act (NCA). This new functionality ensures full NCA compliance while significantly reducing manual effort in managing overdue accounts.
