# 4.69.22 - Summarized Management Report

We have completed the development for a new streamlined reporting solution, the Summarized Mangement Report (SMR), accessible under "**Advanced Reports**" in the **Reporting Module** in Mobiloan.

Combining key insights from four previously separate reports – New Loan Analysis, Receipt Analysis, Client Analysis, and Age Analysis – into a single, clear, and accessible PDF format.
