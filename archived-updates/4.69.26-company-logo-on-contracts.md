# 4.69.26 - Company Logo on Contracts

The company logo will now be added to the footer of all Mobiloan Contract pages. This applies to all contract types, including promissory (A, B, or Single), insurance, affordability expense disclosures, credit report disputes, informal income declarations, and settlement letters. For this to take effect, the company logo must be uploaded in the Company Setup section of Mobiloan.&#x20;
