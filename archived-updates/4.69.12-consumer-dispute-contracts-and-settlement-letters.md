# 4.69.12 - Consumer Dispute Contracts and Settlement Letters

We've simplified the dispute resolution process with the introduction of a signed Consumer Dispute Contract. This contract is automatically generated whenever you make adjustments to credit bureau information during the loan affordability step. You can easily access it from the loan menu for your records.

**Enhanced insurance reporting:** For users with integrated insurance policies, we've added a convenient way to download a comprehensive Insurance Report. This report, available under the Advanced Reporting submenu in the Reporting Module, provides detailed information on the following policies:

* Aspis (issued or premium received policies)
* Groups R Us
* Loanfin (Credit Life or Voluntary Life)

With this update, you can access insightful insurance data with ease, streamlining your reporting and analysis processes.
