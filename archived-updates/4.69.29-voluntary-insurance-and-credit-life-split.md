# 4.69.29 - Voluntary Insurance and Credit Life Split

For improved data accuracy, we've implemented the separation of the Credit Life premium amount from the previously combined Voluntary and Credit Life fields within our database.&#x20;

All reports, contracts, and application tables have been restructured to reflect this change (shown below)
